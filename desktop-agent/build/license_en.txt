WORKPLUS AGENT - MONITORING NOTICE AND AGREEMENT

Please read this before installing. By clicking "I Agree" you confirm that you
have read and understood it.

1. WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES
WorkPlus Agent measures how working time is spent on this computer and sends
the results to your organisation's WorkPlus server. Nothing is collected until
you sign in and confirm your choices in the app's setup screen. After that,
tracking runs only while you are signed in to the WorkPlus website: signing in
there starts it, signing out stops it, and you can pause it at any time on the
website or in this app.

2. WHAT CAN BE COLLECTED (you choose in the app)
- Activity time (required): minutes active and idle, and a count of mouse movements.
- Keyboard activity (optional): the NUMBER of key presses per minute. Never which
  keys, and never anything you type.
- App names (optional): the name of the app in front (for example "Code") and for
  how long. Never window titles, documents, websites or URLs.
- Screenshots (optional, off by default): one picture of your main screen every
  5 minutes while you are active. Never while tracking is paused, you are idle,
  or the screen is locked. A notification appears each time one is taken.

3. WHO CAN SEE YOUR DATA
- Your manager can see your activity totals, app categories, reports and daily
  analyses.
- IF YOU TURN SCREENSHOTS ON, YOUR MANAGER AND YOUR ORGANISATION'S ADMINISTRATORS
  CAN OPEN AND VIEW YOUR SCREENSHOTS. Every time they open one, it is recorded,
  and you can see who viewed it and when.
- Screenshots are also labelled automatically (for example "Coding") by an AI
  service. Only the label is used in reports.

4. YOUR CONTROL
- You will be asked again, inside the app, to choose what to share. Screenshots
  require a separate, explicit agreement that your manager may view them.
- You can pause tracking at any time from the app or the tray icon.
- You can delete any screenshot in the web dashboard.
- You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time in the app's
  Settings. Turning screenshots off deletes all of your screenshots.

5. HOW LONG DATA IS KEPT
- Screenshots are deleted automatically 3 days after they were taken.
- Detailed activity records are deleted after 90 days. Daily totals and reports
  are kept by your organisation.

6. PERMISSIONS
Depending on your choices, your operating system may ask you to allow Screen
Recording or Input Monitoring (macOS). The app only asks for permissions needed
for the items you turned on.

If you do not agree, click "Cancel" (Windows) or "Disagree" (macOS) and the
software will not be installed. Speak to your manager or administrator if you
have questions about how this data is used.
